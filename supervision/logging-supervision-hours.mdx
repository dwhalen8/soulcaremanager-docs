---
title: "The supervision journal"
description: "Log each meeting with your supervising Fellow."
---

The supervision journal is at the bottom of **Practice → You → ACBC certification**. Each meeting you log counts in your progress above it.

## Log a meeting

Click **Log a supervision session** and record:

- the date and how long you met;
- what you discussed (a case conceptualization, say), and any topics and Scripture references.

Write about counselees as you would in a note to your supervisor. The journal is part of your own record, not the counselee's.

## Changing an entry

An entry logged by mistake can be archived with **Archive entry**. **Show archived entries** lists them. Archived entries aren't counted.

## Hours are worked out, not typed

Your supervision hours and meeting count are added up from the journal each time the page opens. *Counted from your acceptance, as your sessions are* (or from your certification, on the road to Fellow).

## It's yours

The journal belongs to the counselor who writes it, and serves your certification alone.

- It isn't part of any counselee's record, and read access to a case never opens it.
- An administrator, or a supervisor with access, can read it only by giving a reason. **Read by someone else**, on the same page, shows who read it, when and why, and Today tells you too.
