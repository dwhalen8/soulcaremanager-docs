**Practice → You → ACBC certification** tracks your progress toward ACBC certification, worked out from your own sessions. It's on a counselor's menu only, and it's **private to you**: nobody else at the practice sees it in their menu, and anyone who reads your supervision journal has to give a reason, which you're shown.

## Setting it up

Click **Certification settings** and fill in:

- **Your supervision status**, which decides what the page counts. It's also on your profile (**Practice → You → Edit profile**).
- **The day ACBC accepted you**: not your first session. Phase 3's year runs from this date.
- **The day ACBC certified you**, once it has. Your hours and two years toward Fellow count from it.
- Your own requirements, only if ACBC set you different ones. Leave them blank for ACBC's standard requirements.

Click **Edit Fellow** to record your **supervising Fellow**: their name and how to reach them. Your certification runs under the Fellow, so the page asks for one first.

## Phase 3

For a counselor in training, the page counts toward ACBC's standard Phase 3 requirements, within a year of your acceptance:

| Meter | Standard requirement |
|---|---|
| **Sessions** | 50 |
| **Counseling hours** | 50 hours of time actually spent, not scheduled slot length |
| **One case** | 10 sessions on a single case (it names the case) |
| **Recordings** | 5 sessions with an audio recording kept for supervisor review |
| **Supervision meetings** | the meetings logged in your journal, and their hours |

Above the meters it says how long you have left (*142 days left — Phase 3 must be complete by …*) and where your current rate will take you.

Every number is worked out from your sessions each time you open the page, never typed in, so there's nothing to keep in step by hand.

### What counts

- **Sessions and hours** come from your completed sessions. A session with no recorded start or end time counts as a session but adds no hours; the page tells you how many, and correcting the session's times adds them.
- **Recordings** count a session recorded in Soul Care Manager, or one where you ticked *This session was audio recorded elsewhere*. See [Recording a session](/sessions/recording-a-session).

## The road to Fellow

Once you're certified, the page counts toward ACBC Fellowship: **counseling hours since certification** (500) and two years of active certification, both from the day ACBC certified you. Phase 3 hours don't count toward Fellow.

ACBC also asks a Fellow candidate for a post-baccalaureate degree, experience teaching biblical counseling, and the supervision of two Phase 3 candidates. The page doesn't track those.
