title: "Signing in"
description: "How to reach your account, and the security steps you'll meet on the way."
---

Go to [soulcaremanager.com/login](https://soulcaremanager.com/login) and enter your email address and password.

If you've saved a passkey on the device you're using, your browser offers it as soon as you click into the email field — pick it and you're signed in, with no password and no code. You can also click **Use a passkey** at any point.

<Note>
  If you belong to more than one practice, you'll be asked which one you're signing in to. That choice is part of signing in, so you make it right on the sign-in card. You can change practices later without signing out.
</Note>

## Your second step

Everyone on staff — counselors, administrators and intake staff — signs in with two things, not one. This isn't optional, and there's no way to switch it off: the records behind it are counseling records.

Your second step is either:

- **An authenticator app** such as Google Authenticator, Authy or 1Password, which shows a six-digit code that changes every 30 seconds; or
- **A passkey** that checks it's really you with a fingerprint, face or device PIN. A passkey of that kind counts as both steps at once, which is why signing in with one takes you straight to the app.

If your account doesn't have either yet, the app stops and sets one up before it lets you any further. You aren't locked out — you're one screen away from being finished.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan the code">
    Open your authenticator app and scan the QR code on screen. If you can't scan, copy the setup key shown beneath it and type it in instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm a live code">
    Type the six-digit code your app is showing. This proves the setup worked before it's saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery codes">
    You'll be shown a short list of recovery codes. **This is the only time you'll see them.** Each one works once, and they're how you get back in if you lose your phone. Save them somewhere that isn't your phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Prefer a passkey? Choose **Use a passkey instead** on the same screen (it sits under the heading **Set up two-factor authentication**). Your browser or phone handles it, and you'll get recovery codes the same way.
</Tip>

## Adding or removing passkeys later

Go to **Practice** and find the **Passkeys** card. From there you can add a passkey for a new device, rename one so you can tell your laptop from your phone, or revoke one you no longer have. Revoking takes effect immediately.

## Two more steps, once each

The first time you sign in, two short screens follow:

- **Your timezone.** Everything you schedule is shown against a clock, so the app asks which one is yours before showing you any times.
- **Anything you need to agree to.** If your practice or the platform has a document awaiting your agreement, it's shown here. Your agreement is recorded with the exact version you read.

Neither appears again unless something changes.

## Forgotten password

Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and enter your email address. You'll get a reset link that's good for a short time.

<Note>
  The page says the same thing whether or not the address has an account. That's deliberate — otherwise the reset form would tell a stranger which of your counselors have accounts.
</Note>

## Staying signed in, and signing out

You stay signed in for about half a day of inactivity, then the app asks for your credentials again. Signing out ends the session immediately on the server, not just in your browser — so if you sign out from a shared computer, that session is genuinely finished.

## Trouble signing in

If you're locked out, your practice administrator can help. If you're the administrator, use the contact option in the app.
