Soul Care Manager is built out of a handful of **workspaces** rather than a long list of pages. You work inside one; detail opens on top of it and closes again, leaving you where you were.

## The workspaces

What you see depends on the role you're signed in as. Nothing is shown greyed-out — if it isn't yours to do, it isn't on your screen.

| Workspace | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Today** | One list of what needs you today — appointments, unsigned notes, overdue homework, anything waiting |
| **Counselees** | The people you care for. Open anyone to reach their record and their case or cases |
| **Session** | The screen you have open during an actual session: timer, notes, homework |
| **Schedule** | The calendar — appointments, repeating series and time off |
| **Practice** | Everything about the practice itself: your team, your profile, branding, the library, forms, billing, reports, settings |
| **My care** | What a counselee sees when they sign in. Staff don't have this one |

<Note>
  Counselors see Today, Counselees, Session, Schedule and Practice. Administrators see the same minus Session — an administrator who doesn't counsel has nothing to do there. Intake staff see Today, Counselees, Schedule and Practice.
</Note>

## Cards, and what's underneath them

Every workspace is built from cards, and every card works the same way: a title, the one action you're most likely to want, a **⋯** menu for the rest, and detail you can expand.

Open something and it slides in as a **drawer** over the workspace rather than sending you to a new page. Close it and you're back where you were, scrolled to the same place. This is why there's no "back to the calendar" trail to follow — you never left.

**Practice** is the biggest of these — one page of cards, and what's on it depends on who you are:

- **Everyone on staff** gets Your profile, Passkeys, Forms, Intake packets, Inquiries, Announcements, Appointment reminders, Booking hours, the Lending library, Account and records, and Legal & privacy.
- **Counselors and administrators** also get the Resource library and Email notifications.
- **Counselors** also get ACBC supervision and ACBC certification. Administrators don't — a counselor's supervision record is their own, not something the practice reads.
- **Administrators** also get Branding, Team, Billing, Subscription, Payments, Reports, Outcome measures, the Audit log and Email history.

Cards you have no business with aren't greyed out, they're absent — which is why an administrator's Practice page and a counselor's look quite different.

## Get anywhere with ⌘K

Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on Windows) from anywhere in the app to open the command palette. Start typing and it finds counselees, screens and actions. Nothing in the app is more than a few keystrokes away, which is often faster than clicking to it.

<Tip>
  The palette only ever offers what your role can actually do. If you can't find an action there, it isn't yours to do rather than hidden somewhere else.
</Tip>

## If you wear more than one hat

Some people hold more than one role at a practice — counselor and administrator, say. Your role is shown in the app, and you can switch between them. Switching changes what you can see and do, so switch to the role you're actually acting as.

If you work at more than one practice, you can switch between practices the same way, without signing out. Only one practice's records are ever on screen at once.

## Times and clocks

**Today** and **Schedule** draw on your own clock, wherever you happen to be. The clinical record — sessions and their corrections — stays on the practice's clock, because that's the time being billed against and read back years later.

That means the same session can read 2:00 on your Schedule and 3:00 on the session record if you're travelling. Nothing is wrong: every screen that shows a session time says which clock it's using.
