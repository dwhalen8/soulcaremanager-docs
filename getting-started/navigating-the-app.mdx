Soul Care Manager is built out of a handful of **workspaces** along the top of the screen, rather than a long list of pages. Everything you work in has its own page and its own web address, so **Back** in your browser takes you up one level, and you can bookmark or share a link to a record (the link only opens for someone allowed to see it).

## The workspaces

What you see depends on the role you're signed in as. Nothing is shown grayed out: if it isn't yours to do, it isn't on your screen.

| Workspace | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Today** | One list of what needs you: sessions to finish, notes to sign, homework turned in, forms submitted, balances owed, prayer requests. Today's schedule is underneath |
| **Counselees** | The people you care for. Pick a name and their record opens beside the list |
| **Session** | The screen you have open during an actual session: timer, notes, homework, and what happened last time |
| **Schedule** | The calendar by day or week: appointments, repeating series and time off |
| **Practice** | Everything about the practice itself and about you: your profile, the team, booking hours, forms, the library, billing, records |
| **My care** | What a counselee sees when they sign in. Staff don't have this one |

<Note>
  Counselors see Today, Counselees, Session, Schedule and Practice. Administrators and intake staff see the same minus Session, because only a counselor runs a session.
</Note>

## Today

Today is where you start. Each row says what's waiting and offers the action that settles it: **Finish**, **Sign**, **Review**, **See balance**. Where the item can be read, there's also **Preview**: the homework, form or prayer request opens in a window over Today with the buttons that settle it, so you can deal with it without leaving the list.

Today also tells you when a Center Admin has read one of your counselees' records, and why. See [Who can see what](/privacy/who-can-see-what).

## A counselee's record

Open **Counselees** and pick someone. Their record opens beside the list (**Hide list** gives it the full width), with tabs along the top:

**Overview** · **Notes** · **Sessions** · **Intake** · **Homework** · **Messages** · **Billing** · **Files** · **People** · **Details**

Each tab has its own address. The Overview shows their cases and *Where things stand*: the next appointment, unsigned notes, outstanding homework and the latest check-in. A case opens in the page body, and a past session opens under its case.

The buttons across the top of the record (**New note**, **Book appointment**, **Message**) are the ones you reach for most. Everything else is in the **⋯** menu beside them.

## Writing beside the record

When you write a note, the record stays in view: the note is on the left and a side panel on the right shows the counselee's intake, homework, past notes and so on, without covering anything. The Session screen works the same way. Your typing is saved as you go, and if you try to leave with something unsaved, the app asks first.

## Practice

**Practice** has a menu down the side, and each section collects the settings and pages that belong together:

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **You** | Your profile, password, passkeys, email notifications, and for a counselor, ACBC certification |
| **Team** | Who works here, each counselor's workload, and branding |
| **Scheduling** | Appointment reminders, booking hours and the practice's time zone |
| **Intake** | Forms, intake packets, inquiries and the website form |
| **Library** | The resource library, the lending library, overdue books, files shared with you, outcome measures |
| **Billing** | Fees and gifts, card payments, tax status, the practice's subscription, and reports |
| **Records** | The audit log, email history, downloading your records, and the legal documents you've agreed to |
| **Announcements** | What the practice and Soul Care Manager have announced |

The sections and pages you see depend on your role. Team and Billing are only on an administrator's menu, and ACBC certification is only on a counselor's.

## Search with ⌘K

Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on Windows), or click **Search** at the top of the screen. Start typing and it finds counselees, pages and actions: *New note for…*, *New appointment for…*, *Booking hours*, *Audit log*. Nothing in the app is more than a few keystrokes away.

<Tip>
  The search only offers what your role can actually do. If an action isn't there, it isn't yours to do, rather than hidden somewhere else.
</Tip>

## If you wear more than one hat

Some people hold more than one role at a practice, counselor and administrator being the usual pair. Your name and current role are at the top right. Open the **▾** menu beside them and choose **Act as Counselor** or **Act as Center Admin**. Switching changes what you can see and do, so switch to the role you're actually acting as. The audit log records which role you were acting in.

If you work at more than one practice, the same menu lists each one, so you can switch without signing out (or type the practice's name into ⌘K). Only one practice's records are ever on screen at once.

## Times and clocks

**Today** and **Schedule** use your own clock, wherever you happen to be. The clinical record (sessions and their corrections) stays on the practice's clock, because that's the time being billed against and read years later.

So the same session can read 2:00 on your Schedule and 3:00 on the session record if you're traveling. Nothing is wrong: every screen that shows a time says which clock it's using, for example *Times shown in America/Chicago*.
