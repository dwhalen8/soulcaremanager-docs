Soul Care Manager is purpose-built for ACBC-certified biblical counselors and the centers where they serve. It handles the day-to-day work of running a counseling practice — keeping track of counselees, scheduling sessions, documenting sessions, sending intake paperwork, assigning homework, and tracking supervision hours — with the language and workflows of biblical counseling rather than clinical mental-health software.

## Who it's for

- **Independent biblical counselors** managing their own caseload
- **Counseling centers** with several counselors working under one roof
- **Counselors in ACBC certification** tracking supervision hours and session counts
- **Counselees**, who get their own private area for appointments, homework and documents

## How records are protected

Two rules shape most of what you'll see in the app, so they're worth knowing before you start:

- **You see your own counselees.** Access follows your caseload. A counselor cannot browse another counselor's records, and that holds inside shared cases too. An administrator who needs to read clinical content has to say why, and the reason is recorded.
- **Clinical records are never destroyed.** Sessions and notes are signed, and after that they're corrected by adding to them rather than by editing them. Counselees are discharged or archived, never deleted. The history stays complete.

## Getting an account

There are two ways in:

- **Join an existing practice.** Your administrator invites you by email address. The invitation arrives as a link; follow it to set up your account.
- **Start your own practice.** Begin a free trial at [soulcaremanager.com/start](https://soulcaremanager.com/start) — no invitation needed.
