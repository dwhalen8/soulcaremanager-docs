<Note>
  This page is for practice administrators. If you're a counselor joining an existing practice, your administrator handles these steps.
</Note>

Everything below lives in the **Practice** workspace, as a card you open and close. Nothing here is buried in a settings menu somewhere else in the app — if it's configuration, it's in Practice.

## 1. Branding

Open the **Branding** card to upload your logo and choose your accent color. Your branding appears on the sign-in page, on emails your counselees receive, and in their portal.

<Note>
  The color picker won't accept a color that's too light to read against white. Contrast isn't a preference here — a counselee with low vision has to be able to read the same screen you can.
</Note>

## 2. Your timezone and booking hours

Open **Booking hours**. Two things live here:

- **Your practice's timezone.** This is the clock your calendar, booking hours and time off are all read against, and it's the clock the clinical record uses. Set it before anyone schedules anything.
- **When the practice offers appointments** — the days and hours you're open. These drive the slots a counselee can pick from when they book themselves in.

Individual counselors can still block out their own time. See [Time off](/scheduling/time-off-blocks).

## 3. Reminders

Open **Appointment reminders** to choose whether counselees are reminded before a session and how far ahead. Reminders never contain anything about the counseling itself — they say there's an appointment and when.

## 4. Intake

Open **Forms** and **Intake packets**. A Personal Data Inventory is included and ready to send, and you can edit it, build other forms, or bundle several documents into a packet you send as one link.

Nothing is sent automatically to a new counselee — you choose what goes out and when.

## 5. Your team

Open the **Team** card to invite counselors, administrators and intake staff.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite by email address">
    Enter the person's email address. The invitation goes to that address and only works there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose their role">
    Counselor, administrator or intake staff. Someone can hold more than one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    They follow the link, set up their account and their second sign-in step, and they're in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Removing someone stamps the date they left, closes their counselee assignments and ends their sessions immediately. Before it goes through, the app shows you which counselees would be left without a counselor so you can reassign them.

<Note>
  A practice can't be left with no administrator. If you're the only one, the app will stop you from removing yourself or taking your own admin role away — add someone else first.
</Note>

## 6. Billing

Open the **Billing** card to see your plan, your trial if you're in one, and what you're paying. See [How billing works](/billing/how-billing-works).

## You're ready

Once branding, booking hours and at least one counselor are in place, counselors can start adding counselees, scheduling sessions and sending intake paperwork.
