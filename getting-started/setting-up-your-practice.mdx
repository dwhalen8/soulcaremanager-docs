<Note>
  This page is for practice administrators. If you're a counselor joining an existing practice, your administrator handles these steps.
</Note>

Everything below lives in the **Practice** workspace, as a card you open and close. Nothing here is buried in a settings menu somewhere else in the app — if it's configuration, it's in Practice.

## 1. Branding

Open the **Branding** card, then **Edit branding**, to choose your accent color and upload your logo (PNG, JPEG, WEBP or SVG, up to 512 KB). Both appear in the app for everyone signed in to your practice — your logo in the corner, your color on buttons and highlights.

Email is the exception: messages from Soul Care Manager are deliberately plain. See [Who can see what](/privacy/who-can-see-what).

<Note>
  The color picker won't accept a color that's too light to read against white. Contrast isn't a preference here — a counselee with low vision has to be able to read the same screen you can.
</Note>

## 2. Your timezone and booking hours

Open **Booking hours** → **Manage booking hours**. Three things live here:

- **This practice's time zone**, under **Booking settings**. This is the clock your calendar, booking hours and time off are all read against, and the clock the counseling record uses. Set it before anyone schedules anything.
- **This practice's hours** — the days and times you're open. These are used by every counselor who hasn't set hours of their own, and they decide what a counselee can pick when they book themselves in.
- **My hours**, where any counselor can choose **Use my own hours** instead, starting from the practice's. Counselors who've done that are listed underneath, so you can see who differs at a glance.

There's a **Preview** on the same drawer that shows what a counselee would actually be offered over the next 14 days, after time off and anything already booked. It's worth a look once you've made changes.

Counselors can also block out their own time. See [Time off](/scheduling/time-off-blocks).

## 3. Reminders

Open **Appointment reminders** to choose how far ahead your counselees are emailed. The card says exactly what goes in one: **date, time and practice name only**. Nothing about the counseling itself is ever emailed.

## 4. Intake

A new practice doesn't start empty. Yours arrives with a Personal Data Inventory, a counseling agreement and a starter resource library already in place, so you can send intake paperwork on day one and change it later.

Open **Forms** and **Intake packets** to read them, edit them, build your own, or bundle several documents into a packet you send as one link.

<Note>
  Nothing goes to a counselee automatically. Someone presses send, every time — an intake link emailed the moment a stranger types an address is an intake link sent to whoever typed it.
</Note>

## 5. Your team

Open the **Team** card and click **Invite a colleague**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter their email address">
    The invitation goes to that address and only works there. If they already use Soul Care Manager, they sign in and accept instead of creating anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose one role">
    **Counselor** holds a caseload of counselees. **Center Admin** runs the practice — its team, settings, billing and records. **Intake** handles inquiries and new counselees' paperwork.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invitation">
    They follow the link, set up their account and their second sign-in step, and they're in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An invitation carries one role. If someone needs two — counselor *and* administrator is the common pair — add the second from the **⋯** menu beside their name once they've joined.

Removing someone stamps the date they left, closes their counselee assignments and ends their sessions immediately. Before it goes through, the app shows you which counselees would be left without a counselor so you can reassign them.

<Note>
  A practice can't be left with no administrator. If you're the only one, the app will stop you from removing yourself or taking your own admin role away — add someone else first.
</Note>

## 6. Money — three separate cards

These are easy to confuse, so it's worth reading once:

| Card | What it's about |
|---|---|
| **Billing** | The standard session fee **this practice charges a counselee**, used whenever a case doesn't have a fee of its own |
| **Payments** | Taking card payments from counselees, through a Stripe account connected to this practice |
| **Subscription** | What this practice pays **us** — the plan you're on, your trial if you're in one, and how to change it |

Set the session fee under **Billing** if you charge one. **Subscription** is where you'd go at the end of a free trial. See [How billing works](/billing/how-billing-works).

## 7. Worth knowing about

Two cards you don't need to configure, but should know are there:

- **Account and records** — you can download a full copy of your practice's records at any time, and previous downloads are listed.
- **Audit log** and **Email history** — who did what and when, including every read of a record, and what the software has emailed, to whom, and whether it arrived.

## You're ready

Once branding, booking hours and at least one counselor are in place, counselors can start adding counselees, scheduling sessions and sending intake paperwork.
