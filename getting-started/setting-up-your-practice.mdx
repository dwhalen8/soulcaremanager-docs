<Note>
  This page is for practice administrators (the **Center Admin** role). If you're a counselor joining an existing practice, your administrator handles these steps.
</Note>

Everything below lives in the **Practice** workspace, under the section named in each heading. Settings are never tucked into the daily screens: if it's configuration, it's in Practice. You can also jump straight to any of these pages with <kbd>⌘K</kbd>.

## 1. Your time zone: Practice → Scheduling

Under **Practice time zone**, choose the zone your practice keeps time in and click **Save time zone**. This is the clock the calendar, booking hours and time off are read against, and the clock the counseling record uses. Set it before anyone schedules anything.

Changing it later doesn't move any appointment, because each one is stored as an exact moment. It does change the times they're shown at.

## 2. Booking hours: Practice → Scheduling → Booking hours

Click **Manage booking hours**. The page has four parts:

- **This practice's hours**: the days and times you're open. They apply to every counselor who hasn't set hours of their own, and they decide what a counselee can pick when they book online.
- **My hours**: any counselor can choose **Use my own hours** instead, starting from the practice's. Counselors who've done that are listed under **Counselors with their own hours**, so you can see who differs at a glance.
- **Preview**: **Preview the next 14 days** shows what a counselee would actually be offered, after time off and anything already booked.
- **Booking settings**: the earliest booking, how far ahead counselees may book, the gap to keep either side of an appointment, the session length, and whether to offer these hours for booking at all.

**Confirm each booking before it is final** is on by default. Requests then wait on your Today list until you accept or decline them. Turn it off only if you want booked times to go straight onto the calendar.

Counselors can also block out their own time. See [Time off](/scheduling/time-off-blocks).

## 3. Reminders: Practice → Scheduling

Open **Reminder settings** to choose how far ahead your counselees are emailed. A reminder carries the **date, time and practice name only**. Nothing about the counseling itself is ever emailed. See [Appointment reminders](/scheduling/appointment-reminders).

## 4. Intake: Practice → Intake

A new practice doesn't start empty. Yours arrives with a Personal Data Inventory (PDI), a counseling agreement and a starter resource library, so you can send intake paperwork on day one and change it later.

- **Forms** holds the questions you ask a new counselee. Read the PDI, edit it, or build your own.
- **Intake packets** bundles several forms and documents, with the counseling agreement, into one link.
- **Inquiries** is where people who fill in your website contact form arrive. **Website form and routing** switches that form on and chooses who unassigned inquiries go to.

<Note>
  Nothing goes to a counselee automatically. Someone presses send every time, because an intake link emailed the moment a stranger types an address goes to whoever typed it.
</Note>

## 5. Your team: Practice → Team

Under **Team**, click **Invite a colleague**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter their email address">
    The invitation goes to that address and only works there. If they already use Soul Care Manager, they sign in and accept instead of creating a new account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose their role">
    **Counselor** holds a caseload of counselees. **Center Admin** runs the practice: its team, settings, billing and records. **Intake** handles inquiries and new counselees' paperwork.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    They follow the link, set up their account and their second sign-in step, and they're in. Until then, the invitation is listed under the team with **Send again** and **Withdraw invitation** in its **⋯** menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An invitation carries one role. If someone needs two (counselor *and* administrator is the usual pair), use **Change roles** in the **⋯** menu beside their name once they've joined.

<Note>
  Only a counselor holds cases. An administrator who also counsels needs both roles; with the Center Admin role alone, they can't be put on a case or a calendar.
</Note>

**Remove from practice**, in the same menu, stamps the date they left, takes them off their cases and ends their sign-ins immediately. Before it goes through, the app shows which counselees would be left with no counselor, and asks what to do with their future appointments: move them to another counselor at the same times, or cancel them.

<Note>
  A practice can't be left with no administrator. If you're the only one, the app won't let you remove yourself or take away your own admin role. Add someone else first.
</Note>

**Workload**, in the same section, compares your counselors side by side: open cases, counselees, sessions this month, the next 30 days and what's waiting on each of them.

## 6. Branding: Practice → Team

Click **Edit branding** to choose your accent color and upload your logo (PNG, JPEG or WEBP, up to 512 KB). Both appear in the app for everyone signed in to your practice: your logo in the corner, your color on buttons and highlights.

Email is the exception. Messages from Soul Care Manager are deliberately plain; see [Who can see what](/privacy/who-can-see-what).

<Note>
  The color picker won't accept a color that's too light to read against white. Contrast isn't a preference here: a counselee with low vision has to be able to read the same screen you can.
</Note>

## 7. Money: Practice → Billing

These cards are easy to confuse, so it's worth reading them once:

| Card | What it's about |
|---|---|
| **Billing** | How sessions are paid at this practice: **by fee** (each session is billed) or **by gift** (no bill is raised, and counselees are invited to give). It holds the standard fee or suggested gift, and whether counselors may set their own |
| **Tax status** | Whether the practice is a registered charity. Every gift acknowledgment follows what you choose here |
| **Payments** | Taking card payments from counselees, through a Stripe account connected to this practice |
| **Card-on-file terms** | The terms a counselee agrees to before saving a card. Until you publish them, nobody is offered the option |
| **Subscription** | What this practice pays **us**: the plan you're on, your trial if you're in one, and how to change or cancel it |
| **Reports** | Caseload, sessions, attendance, discharge outcomes and outstanding money |

See [How billing works](/billing/how-billing-works).

## 8. Worth knowing about: Practice → Records

You don't need to configure anything here, but you should know it's there:

- **Account and records**: download a full copy of your practice's records at any time with **Download all records**.
- **Audit log**: who did what and when, including every read of a record.
- **Email history**: what the software has emailed, to whom, and whether it arrived.
- **Legal & privacy**: the documents you've agreed to, and which version.

## You're ready

Once the time zone, booking hours and at least one counselor are in place, counselors can start adding counselees, scheduling sessions and sending intake paperwork.
