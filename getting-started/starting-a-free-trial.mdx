If you're starting your own practice rather than joining one, you won't need an invitation from anybody. Go to [app.soulcaremanager.com/start](https://app.soulcaremanager.com/start), enter your email address, and we'll send you a link to set your practice up.

<Note>
  No credit card is asked for, and none is needed until the trial ends.
</Note>

## What happens next

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    The link arrives at the address you entered, and only works for that address. If nothing arrives, check your spam folder before trying again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the link within 24 hours">
    The link is good for a day and can be used once. If it expires, just start again at `/start`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your practice">
    Name your practice, create your account and set up your second sign-in step. You'll be signed in when you're done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Nothing exists until you follow the link — if you change your mind and never click it, nothing was created.

## What the trial includes

The trial is the full practice plan for **30 days**. Nothing is held back or watermarked: counselees, cases, scheduling, sessions, notes, intake forms, homework, the portal, supervision tracking and reports all work exactly as they will afterwards.

## When the trial ends

At the end of the 30 days, if you haven't subscribed, the practice becomes read-only: it stops accepting **new** work. Seven things become unavailable:

- a new counselee
- a new case
- a new appointment
- a new repeating appointment
- a new note
- a new session
- a new bill to a counselee

Everything else keeps working. **You don't lose anything, and you aren't locked out:** every record you made is still there and still readable, work you'd already started can still be finished, money you're already owed can still be collected, and subscribing restores the rest immediately. Today and the portal both say when a practice is read-only, and Today offers **Subscribe and restore**.

Your public doors close too while the practice is read-only: the website inquiry form, booking links, and a counselee's new portal messages, prayer requests and bookings.
