If you're starting your own practice rather than joining one, you don't need an invitation from anybody. Go to [soulcaremanager.com/start](https://soulcaremanager.com/start), enter your email address, and we'll send you a link to set your practice up.

<Note>
  No credit card is asked for, and none is needed until the trial ends.
</Note>

## What happens next

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    The link arrives at the address you entered, and only works for that address. If nothing arrives, check your spam folder before trying again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the link within 24 hours">
    The link is good for a day and can be used once. If it expires, just start again at `/start`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your practice">
    Name your practice, create your account and set up your second sign-in step. You'll be signed in when you're done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Nothing exists until you follow the link — if you change your mind and never click it, nothing was created.

## What the trial includes

The trial is the full practice plan for **30 days**. Nothing is held back or watermarked: counselees, cases, scheduling, sessions, notes, intake forms, homework, the portal, supervision tracking and reports all work exactly as they will afterwards.

## When the trial ends

At the end of the 30 days, if you haven't subscribed, the practice stops accepting **new** records. Six things become unavailable:

- a new counselee
- a new case
- a new appointment
- a new repeating appointment
- a new note
- a new session

Everything else keeps working. **You don't lose anything, and you aren't locked out:** every record you made is still there and still readable, work you'd already started can still be finished, and signing up restores the rest immediately.

## If trials aren't open

You may see a message saying free trials aren't open yet. That means the practice-signup route isn't taking payments at the moment — it's not a problem with your address. Get in touch and we'll set you up directly.
