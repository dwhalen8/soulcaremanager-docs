---
title: "Building a form"
description: "Create your own intake forms, surveys and feedback forms."
---

Forms live in **Practice → Intake → Forms**. Every staff member can read them; administrators create and change them.

## Create a form

Click **New form**, give it a name, and choose a category: **Personal data inventory**, **Intake contact**, **Survey**, **Feedback** or **General**. The form opens in the builder, with **What the counselee sees** beside it as you work.

## Pages and questions

A form is a set of pages. Add a page, give it a title, and reorder pages with the arrows. On each page, **Add question** and choose its answer type:

| Answer types | |
|---|---|
| Text | **Short text**, **Long text**, **Number**, **Email address**, **Phone number**, **Website address** |
| Dates | **Date**, **Time** |
| Choices | **Dropdown**, **Dropdown (choose several)**, **Choose one**, **Tick box**, **Rating scale** |
| Composite | **Full name**, **Address**, **List of items** (a small table with your own columns) |
| Agreement | **Agreement to tick** |
| Layout | **Section heading**, **Explanatory text**, **Page break**, **Hidden value** |

Each question can have **Help text** and can be marked **Required**. Choice questions take their choices one per line. You can also choose how wide a question sits on the page, so short questions can share a row.

## Showing a question only when it applies

A question can depend on earlier answers: **Show this only when all of these are true**, then choose the earlier question, the condition and the answer to match. "If married, how long?" only appears for someone who said they're married.

## Filling in the record

**Fills in** connects a question to a field on the counselee's record, such as date of birth or phone number. When the form comes back, that answer fills the record's field if it's blank, or is offered as a change if it differs.

## Charging for a form

If you charge for intake, set an **Intake price** on the form. A counselee who submits it is billed automatically and given a link to pay. Leave it empty to ask for nothing.

## Saving and versions

Click **Save form**. If a counselee has already answered this version, saving creates a new version, and their answers keep the questions they were asked.

Required answers are checked when the form is submitted, so a form can't come back missing something you've marked required.
