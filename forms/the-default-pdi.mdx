---
title: "The Personal Data Inventory"
description: "The intake form every new practice starts with, and how it fills in a counselee's record."
---

Every practice starts with a **Personal Data Inventory (PDI)**, the standard biblical-counseling intake form: nine pages covering personal details, health, religious background, marriage and family, and what brings them to counseling. It's ready to send on day one.

Find it in **Practice → Intake → Forms**, marked *default personal data inventory*.

## Sending it

The PDI goes out when someone presses send. Nothing is emailed automatically.

- When you **add a counselee** with an email address, the PDI is already chosen under **Intake form to email**, and **Add and email the form** sends it in the same press. See [Adding a counselee](/counselees/adding-a-counselee).
- For someone already on file, open their record's **Intake** tab and click **Send a form**. See [Sending a form](/forms/sending-a-form).

## The agreement at the end

A PDI ends with your practice's **counseling agreement**, if the counselee hasn't signed the current version yet. They type their name to sign, and the signature is recorded with the exact version they read. If your practice records sessions, the same step shows the [counselee terms](/sessions/recording-a-session) and the one signature covers both.

Edit the agreement in **Practice → Intake → Intake packets**. **Publish new version** supersedes the current one; earlier versions are kept, so a counselee's signature always points at the text they actually signed.

## It fills in the record

When the PDI comes back, it fills in the counselee's record for you: date of birth, phone, address and the other details it asks for. It fills **blank** fields only. Where an answer differs from what's already on file, the submission says *These answers differ from what is on file. Nothing changes until you choose*, and you pick which changes to take.

## Changing it

Open the PDI from **Forms** to change its questions. See [Building a form](/forms/building-a-custom-form).

Once a counselee has answered it, saving your changes makes a **new version**. Their answers keep the questions they were actually asked, so an old submission always reads correctly.

If your practice has more than one personal data inventory (one carried over from your earlier records, say), choose the one to send by default with **Make default personal data inventory**. A form you no longer use can be **Retired**.

## How long the link works

A form link works for **14 days**, and only for the person it was sent to. After that, send it again. See [Sending a form](/forms/sending-a-form).
