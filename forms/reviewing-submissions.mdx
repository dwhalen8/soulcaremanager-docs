---
title: "Reviewing submissions"
description: "Read what came back, annotate it, update the record from it, and share it."
---

A submitted form appears on **Today** (*Personal Data Inventory (PDI) · submitted … ago*, with **Preview** and **Review**) and on the counselee's **Intake** tab. Each form says which case it's filed under.

## Reading it

Open the form to read every answer, page by page. **Preview** on Today opens it in a window over the list.

Answers are encrypted, so they can't be searched by their content. Filter by counselee or form instead.

## Annotating

Select part of an answer (or the whole answer) and click **Annotate** to add a question or note, and an answer to it if you have one. Annotations are for you and the counselors on the case; the counselee never sees them, and the answer itself is never changed.

## Updating the record

If the answers differ from what's on the counselee's record, the submission lists them: *These answers differ from what is on file. Nothing changes until you choose.* Pick the changes to take.

## The counselee's answers are theirs

You can't change what a counselee wrote. If something is wrong or unclear, annotate it, or ask them about it. **Edit answers** appears only on a form you filled in yourself.

## Download a PDF

**Download PDF** gives you a copy of the submitted form. The PDF is made when the form is submitted and kept with the record, behind sign-in. It's never emailed to anyone.

## Sharing with another counselor

To show a form to a counselor who isn't on the case, share it from the form with a note. The share opens that one form and nothing else of the person's record, and it's recorded as a disclosure. You can **Revoke** it at any time.

## Removing a form

**Remove form** takes a form off the counselee's record (a form sent to the wrong person, say). It no longer appears anywhere in the app, and it can't be put back from there, so the app asks you to confirm first.

## Who can see it

- The counselors on the counselee's cases read it.
- An administrator reads it only by giving a reason, which is recorded. They can't annotate or share it.
- Intake staff see that it came back, never the answers.
