---
title: "Sending a form"
description: "Email a form, send a whole packet, or fill one in yourself."
---

Forms go out from a counselee's record, on the **Intake** tab.

## Send one form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Send a form">
    Under **Intake links**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the form">
    Under **Form to send**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how it goes">
    - **Email to …** sends it to the email address on their record.
    - **Copy link instead** gives you the link to send another way.
    - **Fill in myself** opens the form for you to complete with them, in a session or on the phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the counselee is on a case, the form is filed on that case, and you can choose more than one.

## Send a packet

An **intake packet** bundles several forms, the counseling agreement and any documents a new counselee needs to read into one link. On the **Intake** tab, under **Intake packets**, click **Send a packet**.

Administrators build packets in **Practice → Intake → Intake packets** (**Add packet**, then **Edit items**).

## What the counselee receives

An email that says only that a form is waiting, with the link. No name, no practice details, nothing about counseling. Everything they fill in stays behind the link.

They can save and come back; their answers are kept as they go. When they submit, you get an email saying a form has come back (if you've switched that on in **Practice → You → Email notifications**), and the form appears on **Today** for you to review.

## How long the link works

A form link works for **14 days**. Each link is for one person and one form; it's rate-limited, and every time it's used is recorded.

The record's **Intake links** list shows each link and where it stands. **Revoke** stops a link at once. **Send again** makes a new link to the same form or packet (keeping anything already filled in) and stops the old one: use it when a link has run out or the email went astray.

## One writer at a time

While a link is out with the counselee, only they can write the form. If you need to fill it in yourself, revoke their link first. That way two people are never typing into the same form.

## Intake staff

Intake staff can send forms and see whether they've come back, but they never see the answers.
