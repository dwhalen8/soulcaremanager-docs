---
title: "Appointment reminders"
description: "Automatic reminder emails before each appointment, and what they say."
---

Soul Care Manager emails each counselee a reminder before their appointment. A reminder gives **the date, time and practice name only**: never a name, a location or what the appointment is about.

## How far ahead

Go to **Practice → Scheduling** and click **Reminder settings**. **Send reminders this many hours ahead** is your own setting, between 1 and 168 hours (one week), and applies to your own counselees.

An administrator also sees **Send appointment reminders for this practice**. Turning it off silences reminders for everyone at the practice, whatever each counselor has set.

## One counselee at a time

On a counselee's record, the **Details** tab has **Send appointment reminder emails**. Untick it and that counselee gets no reminders. A counselee with a portal login can change this themselves in their portal profile.

This switch is for reminders only. A cancelled or moved appointment, a booking answer and a payment link still go to the email on file.

## Sending one early

Open the appointment on the Schedule. Under **Reminder**, the panel says whether it has gone yet, and **Send reminder now** sends it straight away.

## When a time changes

Moving an appointment resets its reminder, so the counselee is reminded of the new time, not the old one.

## Checking what went out

The **Email history** card on the counselee's **Details** tab lists what was sent to them and whether it was delivered, without the message itself. Administrators can see every email in **Practice → Records → Email history**.

## Text message reminders

Text reminders are built but switched off for now. They'll be available once the phone carriers approve Soul Care Manager's registration. Until then, the texting options don't appear anywhere in the app.
