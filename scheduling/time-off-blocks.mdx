---
title: "Time off"
description: "Block out vacations, study time and anything else you're not available for."
---

A **blockout** marks time you're not available. It shows on your calendar as **Blocked**, and it removes that time from what counselees can book online.

## Add a blockout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New blockout">
    On the **Schedule**, above the calendar or under **Time off**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a label">
    Your own note, such as *Vacation* or *Study block*. Never a counselee's details: colleagues who can see your calendar see that you're busy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when">
    **From** and **To (inclusive)**, and either **All day** or a start and end time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it to repeat (optional)">
    Tick **Repeats** for something regular, like a weekly study morning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save" />
</Steps>

Your blockouts are listed under **Time off** below the calendar. Click one to change it, or **Archive** it if the time off is no longer happening.

## Repeating time off

A repeating blockout is stored as a rule in the practice's time zone ("every Friday morning"), so it stays at the same wall-clock time through daylight-saving changes.

## Appointments already booked

Adding time off doesn't cancel anything already on your calendar. If an appointment falls inside it, the calendar shows both, so you can move or cancel the appointment yourself. When you book something new that clashes with time off, the app tells you first.
