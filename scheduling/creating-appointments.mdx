The **Schedule** shows your calendar by **Day**, **Week** or **Month**, with **←**, **Today** and **→** to move through it. The line at the top, *Times shown in America/Chicago*, says which clock the calendar is drawn on: yours. See [Which clock is which](/scheduling/which-clock).

A repeating appointment shows **↻** beside its name, and your time off shows as **Blocked**.

## Book an appointment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New appointment">
    Above the calendar, or the **+** on any day to start on that date. From a counselee's record, **Book appointment** does the same with them already chosen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the date, start time and length">
    The start time is in the practice's time zone, and the box says which (*Start (America/Chicago)*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the counselee and case">
    Start typing a name to find a counselee. **Case** lists your open cases, including couple and family cases, so you can book a joint appointment by choosing the shared case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a location or video link (optional)">
    A **Video call link** is shown to the counselee in their portal and on your own calendar feed, with a **Join call** button on the appointment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it to repeat (optional)">
    Tick **Repeats** and choose **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Yearly**, how often (**Every** 2 weeks, say), and when it ends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Schedule">
    If the time clashes with another appointment or your time off, the app tells you before booking, and lets you book anyway if you mean to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Repeating appointments

A repeating appointment is stored as a rule ("every Tuesday at 10:00"), not as a long list of copies. Each occurrence appears on the calendar, and becomes an appointment of its own the moment you change it. That's why a repeating series stays at the same wall-clock time across daylight-saving changes.

## Opening an appointment

Click an appointment and it opens in a panel beside the calendar, which stays live. The panel shows when it is, the counselor, its status, the location, who's attending and how it repeats. From there:

- **Edit** changes the time, length, location or counselee. On a repeating appointment, you choose **This appointment only** or **This and all following**.
- **Status** marks it **Scheduled**, **Completed**, **No-show** or cancelled.
- **Send reminder now** sends the reminder early, if it hasn't gone yet.
- **Counts as a full session** decides how it's numbered on the case: **Automatic** (a whole number when everyone on the case was there), **Yes** or **No** (a part number, such as 2.1).
- **End series…**, in the **⋯** menu, stops a repeating appointment from a date you choose.

## What the counselee is told

- **Moving** a scheduled appointment to a new time that's still ahead emails the counselee the new time. Changing the whole series sends one email, not one per occurrence.
- **Cancelling** asks you to confirm, because each counselee with an email address on file is emailed that it was cancelled. Putting it back to Scheduled later doesn't unsend that email.

Neither email says anything about the counseling: only the date, time and practice.

## Nothing is deleted

Cancelled appointments stay on the calendar's history so the record is complete. There's no way to delete one.

## Bookings from counselees

If your practice offers online booking, counselees can book from a link or their portal, during your [booking hours](/scheduling/setting-your-availability). A booking is always made on one of the counselee's open cases, with that case's counselor. If **Confirm each booking before it is final** is on, the request waits on your **Today** list as a **Booking request** until you **Confirm** or **Decline** it.
