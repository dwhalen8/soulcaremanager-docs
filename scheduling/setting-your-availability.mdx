title: "Setting your availability"
description: "Booking hours decide what a counselee can pick when they book themselves in."
---

**Booking hours** are the days and times counselees can book online. Booking hours don't stop *you* from booking anything: you can put an appointment at any time from the Schedule. They govern what a counselee is offered.

Find them in **Practice → Scheduling → Booking hours** (or press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> and type *Booking hours*).

## The practice's hours and your own

- **This practice's hours** are used by every counselor who hasn't set hours of their own. An administrator sets them.
- **My hours** starts out saying *You are using the practice's hours*. Choose **Use my own hours** to set your own, starting from a copy of the practice's.

Each set of hours is a list of bands: a day, a start and an end, in the practice's time zone. Add one with **Add hours**. To take Wednesday afternoons off, for example, give Wednesday a band that ends at noon.

**Counselors with their own hours** lists everyone who has set their own, with **Change** and **Remove** for each band.

## Check what counselees will see

**Preview the next 14 days** shows exactly what a counselee would be offered, after your [time off](/scheduling/time-off-blocks) and anything already booked. It's the quickest way to check a change worked.

## Booking settings

An administrator sets these for the practice:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Earliest booking, in hours from now** | Stops a counselee booking a slot that starts too soon |
| **How far ahead counselees may book, in days** | How far into the future slots are offered |
| **Gap to keep either side of an appointment, in minutes** | Leaves time between sessions |
| **Session length, in minutes** | The length of each slot offered |
| **Offer these hours for booking** | Switches online booking on or off |
| **Confirm each booking before it is final** | Requests wait on your Today list until you accept or decline them |

Times are offered in the practice's time zone. Check it's right (**Practice → Scheduling**) before switching booking on.

## Who a counselee can book with

A counselee books with the counselor who holds their case, on one of their open cases. Someone with no open case can't book online yet; book their first appointment yourself.
