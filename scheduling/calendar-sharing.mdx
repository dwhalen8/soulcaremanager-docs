---
title: "Calendar sharing and subscriptions"
description: "See your sessions in your own calendar app, and let colleagues see when you're busy."
---

On the **Schedule**, under **Sharing & subscriptions**, click **Manage**. There are two separate things here.

## Subscribe from your calendar app

A **calendar subscription** is a read-only feed of your own schedule for Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create subscription URL" />
  <Step title="Copy the address">
    Paste it into your calendar app's "subscribe to a calendar" or "add from URL" option.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The feed shows each session's time and never a counselee's name or a location. Each session links back to its prep page, which opens only once you've signed in.

- **Show counselees' initials** makes sessions read *Session – J.R.* instead of *Counseling session*. Your calendar company, and anyone you share that calendar with, will see the initials too, so it's off unless you turn it on.
- **Regenerate** makes a new address and stops the old one working at once. Use it if a phone or laptop is lost.
- **Turn off** stops the feed entirely.

The panel shows when the address expires and when it was last used. *Never used* usually means it was never pasted into a calendar app. Subscription addresses expire after about 13 months, so you'll occasionally need to create a new one.

## Let colleagues see when you're busy

Under **Who can see when you are busy**, choose a colleague and click **Share**. They see your busy times and nothing else: no counselee, no case, no location.

**Hide appointments I have marked private** leaves your private appointments off what they see. (Mark an appointment private when you edit it.)

**Shared with you** lists colleagues who've shared their calendars with you. On the Schedule, the **⋯** menu shows or hides their busy times beside yours.

When someone leaves the practice, every share they gave or received ends.
