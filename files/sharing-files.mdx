---
title: "Sharing files"
description: "Show one document to a colleague who can't otherwise see the record."
---

Sometimes a colleague needs to see one document (a release form, a report) without having access to the counselee's whole record. Share just that file.

## Share a file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Sharing beside the file" />
  <Step title="Choose a colleague">
    Under **Share with**, and add a note saying why (optional).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share" />
</Steps>

The share opens that single document and nothing else of the person's record. **Sharing** beside the file lists who it's shared with, and you can revoke each share at any time.

## What the colleague sees

The file appears in their **Practice → Library → Shared with you** card. *A share opens that single document and nothing else of the person's record.*

## It's recorded

Every share is recorded in the audit log as a disclosure: what was shared, by whom, with whom and when.

## When someone leaves

If the colleague leaves the practice, every share made to them ends.
