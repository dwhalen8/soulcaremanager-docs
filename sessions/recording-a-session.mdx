---
title: "Recording a session"
description: "Recording audio, the counselee terms it needs, and how long the audio is kept."
---

You can record a session's audio from the session page, to review it with your supervisor or, once transcription is available, to have it transcribed.

## Before you can record: the counselee terms

Recording needs **everyone in the session** to have acknowledged Soul Care Manager's current **counselee terms**, which cover session recording. Until they have, the **Recording** section says who is still to acknowledge them.

A counselee can acknowledge the terms:

- at the end of their intake form, with the same signature as your counseling agreement;
- in their portal; or
- with you, in the session. If they've read the terms with you, click **Record acknowledgement** for them.

For a counselee under 18 (by the birth date on their record), only a parent or guardian's acknowledgment counts, and staff record it.

When the terms are updated, everyone acknowledges the new version before the next recording. Withdrawing an acknowledgment is done by staff, from the counselee's **Details** tab. A counselee who objects talks to their counselor, rather than withdrawing it in the portal.

<Note>
  An administrator can require the terms for the whole portal: **Practice → Intake → Recording terms**. A counselee who hasn't agreed to them then sees only the terms in their portal until they do.
</Note>

## Recording

Click **Record this session**. When the session ends, click **Stop recording**, or **Finish recording** to save it.

If the browser you're using can't record, the section says so: *This browser can't record here. Upload a recording made on another device.* Use **Upload a recording** instead.

The audio is encrypted as it's stored, and played back only to people signed in who can read the session. Only the counselor who recorded it can download it, and each download is recorded in the audit log.

## Recorded somewhere else

If you recorded on a phone or another device and kept the recording outside Soul Care Manager, tick **This session was audio recorded elsewhere**. It counts toward the recordings ACBC asks you to review with your supervisor. A recording made or uploaded here counts by itself. See [ACBC progress tracking](/supervision/acbc-progress-tracking).

## How long the audio is kept

The audio is a working copy, not the permanent record:

| | Audio kept for |
|---|---|
| A recording that has been transcribed | 15 days after the transcript |
| A recording that hasn't been transcribed | 30 days |
| A recording that was never finished | 30 days from when it started |

After that, the audio is erased. The session's record says that a recording existed, and the transcript (if there is one) stays as part of the record. A legal hold on the practice or on the counselee keeps the audio until the hold is lifted.

## Transcripts and summary drafts

<Note>
  Transcription and summary drafts aren't switched on yet. This section describes how they'll work.
</Note>

- **Transcribe** sends a finished recording to the transcription service. The transcript comes back with speakers labeled and is kept, as it came and never edited, as part of the session. What it got wrong belongs in your note. The transcription service deletes its copy as soon as the transcript is collected.
- **Draft a summary** asks for a summary of the transcript. Only the counselor who ran the session can ask. The draft is marked *Machine-drafted — check every line against the transcript before you keep any of it*, and it isn't saved anywhere until you add it: to the shared note while the session is open, or as a new addendum once it's locked. Only lines of the transcript are sent, with speakers named by role ("Counselor", "Counselee 1"), never by name.
