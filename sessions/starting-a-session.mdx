---
title: "Running a session"
description: "Prep, start, document and complete a counseling session."
---

The **Session** workspace is the screen you have open during an actual session: a timer, the lesson, your notes for the room and for each counselee, homework, and what happened last time, all on one page. Only counselors have it.

## Before: session prep

Open the appointment (on **Today** or the **Schedule**) and click **Prep**. *Session prep* shows the appointment and, beside it, a **Context** column: **Last session**, **Earlier notes**, **Intake**, **Care plan**, **Homework** and **Safety notes**.

*Nothing here starts the session or its timer. Read as long as you like.*

When you're ready, click **Start session**.

## A session that was never booked

For a walk-in or a phone call, click **Start ad hoc session**, on the **Session** workspace or on the case's page. It records against a case you already hold, with no appointment behind it.

## During the session

The session page has the same **Context** column on the right, so you can look back without leaving. On the left, from top to bottom:

- **Recording**, if you're recording. See [Recording a session](/sessions/recording-a-session).
- **The timer**, with **Stop timer** when the session ends and **Adjust times** if it started or ended at a different time.
- **Lesson**: what you're teaching today. Picking a lesson from your library copies its text here so you can adapt it; your changes stay on this session and never touch the original.
- **Shared note**: what was said in front of everyone.
- **Each counselee's own record**: their weekly check-in (1–10) or other outcome measure, a narrative for that person, and the homework they're due to discuss.

Everything you type saves as you go. If you try to leave with something unsaved, the app asks first.

<Note>
  In a couple or family session, each person's record is their own. See [Joint sessions](/sessions/joint-sessions).
</Note>

## Homework in the session

The homework each counselee is due to discuss is listed under their record. When they've turned something in, you can **Confirm done**; if they haven't, you can mark it done yourself, or leave it open. You can also assign new homework here. See [Lessons and homework](/resources/lessons-and-homework).

## Completing the session

When you've finished writing, click **Complete session** and confirm with **Complete & lock**. The session is now part of the permanent record: it can't be edited, only added to.

<Card title="Signing and corrections" icon="signature" href="/sessions/signing-and-corrections">
  What locking means, and how to correct a locked session.
</Card>

You don't have to complete a session the moment the counselee leaves. A session that has ended but isn't complete waits on **Today** (*Ended … — still not completed*, with **Finish**) and at the top of the **Session** workspace under **In progress**, with **Resume**.

## Reading a past session

Every session is listed on its case page, numbered for you (**Session 1**, **Session 2**…), and on the record's **Sessions** tab. Opening one shows the locked record: when it was, its status (*Complete · locked*), the counselor, the lesson, the shared note and each counselee's record. **Open in Session workspace** opens it beside the Context column.
