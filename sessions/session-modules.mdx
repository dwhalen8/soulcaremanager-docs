## Lessons

A **lesson** is teaching material from your resource library. In a session, choose one under **Lesson** (**Search lessons**). Its text is copied into the session so you can adapt it for this counselee; your changes stay on this session and never touch the library copy.

If a lesson comes with worksheets, using it shows each one first, ticked, under **Use this lesson**, so you can see exactly what will be added to the session before anything is. Untick what you don't want.

Add and edit lessons in **Practice → Library → Resource library**. See [The resource library](/resources/the-resource-library).

## The weekly check-in

Every counselee's record in a session has a **Weekly check-in (1–10)**: how their week went, where a higher score is better. Scores build up into a trend you can see on the counselee's record (the Overview's *Latest check-in*).

## Other outcome measures

An administrator can add more scales in **Practice → Library → Outcome measures** (**Add a measure**): a standard questionnaire such as a PHQ-9, or anything your practice tracks. Each one appears on every counselee's record and on their trend chart, and counselors score it during a session.

A measure that's no longer used can be **Retired**. Its past scores stay on the record.

## Correcting a score

A score recorded in a completed session is part of the locked record. If it was wrong, file a **Correction** addendum on the session. The original and the correction are both kept. See [Signing and corrections](/sessions/signing-and-corrections).

## Session numbers

Sessions are numbered on each case automatically. An appointment's **Counts as a full session** setting decides how: **Automatic** gives a whole number when everyone on the case attended, and a part number (such as 2.1) otherwise. Choose **Yes** or **No** on the appointment to decide yourself. The numbers are worked out fresh each time, so changing one renumbers the sessions after it.
