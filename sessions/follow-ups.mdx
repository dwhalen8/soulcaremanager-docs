title: "Care plans"
description: "The goals a case is working towards, what you're doing about each, and how it's going."
---

A **care plan** belongs to a case. It holds a formulation (your understanding of what's going on) and a set of **goals**, each with what you're doing about it and notes on how it's going.

## Start a plan

On the case page, under **Care plan**, click **Start a care plan**. The plan starts empty, dated today. Click **Edit** under **Formulation** to write yours.

## Add goals

Click **Add a goal** to write your own, or choose from **Apply a template…** to start from one of your practice's templates, such as *Put off / put on*, *Heart desires and motivations*, *Anger*, *Anxiety and fear*, *Marriage: communication and conflict*, *Grief and suffering* or *Forgiveness and reconciliation*.

A template's goals open **ticked** under **Add N goals**, so you can read each one, untick what doesn't fit, and change the wording before anything is added. Nothing is written until you press it. The goals are copied into the plan, so editing them later never changes the template.

For each goal you can add **interventions** (what you're doing about it) and **progress** notes as the case goes on.

## In the session

The plan is one of the **Context** tabs on session prep and the session page, so it's in front of you while you counsel.

## Changing your mind

A goal that was added by mistake can be taken out with **Remove**. It isn't deleted: **Show removed goals** lists it, with **Restore** to bring it back, so the plan's history stays complete.

## Care plans and homework

| | Care plan | Homework |
|---|---|---|
| **Who it's for** | You and anyone counseling on the case | The counselee |
| **What it holds** | Goals, interventions, progress | Reading, worksheets and tasks to do between sessions |
| **Does the counselee see it?** | No | Yes, in their portal |
