---
title: "Inviting counselees to the portal"
description: "Give a counselee their own sign-in, and take it away again."
---

A counselee only gets a portal account when you invite them. Today reminds you about counselees who don't have one (*Marcus Pérez has no portal account*).

## Send an invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open their record's Details tab">
    Find **Portal access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite to portal">
    The invitation goes to the email address on their record, and only there. Check it's right first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They follow the link">
    They choose a password and they're in. The link works for 14 days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until they use it, **Portal access** says the invitation is waiting, with **Resend invitation** if it went astray or ran out.

A counselor can invite only their own counselees.

## Once they're in

**Portal access** reads *Active — their email. He can sign in and see his own appointments and documents.* Their record's header says **Portal account** too.

They sign in at [app.soulcaremanager.com](https://app.soulcaremanager.com/login) with the email address their invitation went to.

## Withdrawing access

**Withdraw access**, in the same place, stops them signing in to your practice's portal. Their record and everything in it stays; only the sign-in goes.

## If your practice requires the counselee terms

An administrator can require Soul Care Manager's counselee terms for the whole portal (**Practice → Intake → Recording terms**). A counselee who hasn't acknowledged them then sees only the terms in their portal until they do.
