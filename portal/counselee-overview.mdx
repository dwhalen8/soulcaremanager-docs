---
title: "What counselees see"
description: "My care: the counselee's own page for appointments, homework, documents, messages and their account."
---

A counselee with a portal account signs in at the same address you do, [app.soulcaremanager.com](https://app.soulcaremanager.com/login), with their email and password, and lands on **My care**. They see only what's theirs, at your practice.

<Note>
  A counselee signs in with a password (or a passkey) and no second step. The second step is for staff, who can see other people's records.
</Note>

## What's on My care

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Announcements** | What your practice has posted for counselees |
| **Messages** | Their conversation with their counselors. See [Messages](/portal/messages) |
| **Prayer requests** | What they've asked prayer for. See [Prayer requests](/portal/prayer-requests) |
| **Book an appointment** | Available times with their counselor, if your practice offers online booking |
| **Appointments** | Their upcoming appointments for the next year, with **Join call** for a video session |
| **My homework** | Homework assigned to them, what their counselor wrote about it, and whether it's done |
| **Forms you've completed** | The intake forms they've sent you |
| **My documents** | What they've signed or acknowledged (your counseling agreement, the counselee terms), with **Read** to see the exact version |
| **My account** | What they owe, and **Download my statement**. With card payments set up, they can pay here |
| **My profile** | Their details on file, notifications, password and passkeys |

## Times are your practice's

Every time in the portal is on your practice's clock, and the page says so: *Times are Wellspring Biblical Counseling's local time (Central Time)*, and what their own device is set to if it differs.

## What counselees never see

- Your notes, session narratives, care plans and safety notes.
- Annotations on their forms.
- Anything about anyone else, including others on a couple or family case: each person sees their own homework, documents and messages.

## Their profile

**My profile** shows the details your practice has on file (name, email, phone, date of birth, address), read-only: *To change any of it, ask your practice.* There they can also:

- switch **Email me appointment reminders** on or off;
- **Change password**, and add or remove passkeys;
- see when they acknowledged the counselee terms.

## When your practice is read-only

If your practice's subscription lapses, My care says so, and new messages, prayer requests and bookings pause until it's restored. Everything already there stays readable.
