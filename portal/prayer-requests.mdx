---
title: "Prayer requests"
description: "Counselees can ask for prayer from the portal, privately or shared with their counselors."
---

A counselee can send a prayer request from **My care**, under **Prayer requests**, with an optional subject.

## Shared or private

- **Shared**: their counselors can read it and pray for them. It appears on the counselee's record and on your **Today** list.
- **Private**: they tick *Keep this private — nobody at the practice will see it, not even an administrator.* It stays in their own list only.

They can see all their requests, each marked **Shared** or **Private**.

## On your side

A shared request appears on **Today** (*Prayer request · … ago*, with **Preview** and **Open**) and on the record's **Messages** tab, under **Prayer requests**.

You can mark a request **answered**. That's your own record: the counselee isn't told anything beyond the status. To write to them about it, use [Messages](/portal/messages).

## Who can read shared requests

The counselors on the counselee's cases. Read access to a case never opens prayer requests.
