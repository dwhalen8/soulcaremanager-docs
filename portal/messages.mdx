---
title: "Messages"
description: "Secure messages between a counselee and their counselors."
---

Messages are a counselee's private conversation with their counselors, inside Soul Care Manager. Nothing in a message is ever emailed.

## Writing to a counselee

On the counselee's record, click **Message** (or open the **Messages** tab), write, and click **Send**.

You can message a counselee who has a working portal account and an open case with you. If they can't receive one, the tab says why.

## What they're told

If they have nothing unread waiting, they're emailed that a message is waiting, never what it says. They sign in to read it.

## Their side

On **My care**, under **Messages**, they see the conversation and can write back. The box tells them:

*Your counselors can read this, and it becomes part of your counseling record. It is not monitored between appointments — if you need help now, contact your practice directly or call emergency services.*

When they write, you see it on the record's **Messages** tab. Your own messages say **Read** or **Unread**, so you know whether they've seen them.

Times on the Messages tab are on the practice's clock, as everywhere in the record, and the tab says so if your own clock differs. The counselee sees the same times in their portal.

## It's part of the record

Messages are kept as part of the counselee's counseling record. They can't be edited or deleted after sending.

## Who can read them

The counselors on the counselee's cases. An administrator only with a reason, which is recorded.
