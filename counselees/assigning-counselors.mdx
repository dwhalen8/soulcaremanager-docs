---
title: "Assigning counselors"
description: "Who holds a case, how that changes, and who can see what as a result."
---

In Soul Care Manager, **a counselor sees a counselee because a case puts them together.** There's no "counselor" field on the counselee's profile to edit: the case's **Counselor of record** decides whose caseload a counselee is on.

## Primary and co-counselor

Every case has one **primary** counselor, who holds it. A case can also have **co-counselors**, for example when two counselors see a couple together. A co-counselor can read the case's sessions and notes, but each counselor writes only their own.

Only someone with the **Counselor** role can be on a case. An administrator who also counsels needs both roles.

## Assigning a counselor

On the case page, under **Counselor of record**, click **Assign counselor**, choose the person, and choose primary or co-counselor.

- **Setting the primary counselor** is an administrator's decision, because it moves the case from one caseload to another.
- **A counselor can add a co-counselor** to their own case.
- **Nobody assigns themselves** except an administrator. A counselor can't put themselves on someone else's case.

When a new primary takes over, the previous one comes off the case.

## History, not overwrites

Assignments are never edited in place. Each one has a start and, when it ends, an end, so the case always shows who held it and when. A counselor who comes off a case stops seeing it from that moment; everything they wrote stays in the record under their name.

## Future appointments go with the change

If the counselor coming off a case has appointments still to come on it, the app asks what to do with them before it goes through:

- **Move them** to another counselor, keeping the same times (checked for clashes on the new counselor's calendar), or
- **Cancel them**, which ends any repeating series and tells the counselee.

It won't go through until you choose. The same question comes up when someone is removed from the practice or loses the Counselor role.

## Read access for another counselor

Sometimes a counselor needs to read a case they don't hold: covering for a colleague, or a case review. An administrator can open the case's **⋯** menu and choose **Give a counselor read access**.

<Warning>
  Read access opens the **case record only**: its focus, roster, counselors and discharge. It never opens the case's notes, sessions, files, measurements, homework, care plan, messages or prayer requests, and never a counselor's supervision journal. Those stay with the counselors who hold the case.
</Warning>

Access can be revoked from the same place at any time.

## Who can see which counselees

- **Counselors** see the counselees on their own cases, and nobody else.
- **Center Admins** see every counselee's record, but reading clinical content (notes, sessions, safety notes) asks for a reason. The reason is recorded, and the counselors are told. See [Who can see what](/privacy/who-can-see-what).
- **Intake staff** see records and send forms, but never clinical content or form answers.

Because access follows the case, reassigning a case changes whose list a counselee appears on immediately.
