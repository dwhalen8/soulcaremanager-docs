---
title: "Adding a counselee"
description: "Start a record with a name and an email, and let the intake form fill in the rest."
---

A counselee's record starts small: a name, and usually an email address. The intake form they fill in completes the rest of it. There are three ways someone arrives:

- **You add them**, from **Counselees**.
- **They fill in your website inquiry form**, and arrive in **Practice → Intake → Inquiries**. See [Inquiries](/counselees/inquiries).
- **They fill in a counselor's own inquiry link**, and arrive straight on that counselor's caseload.

## Add a counselee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Counselees">
    Click **Counselees** at the top of the screen, then **Add counselee** above the list. (Or press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> and type *Add counselee*.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their name, email and phone">
    Only the first and last name are required. Give an email address if you'll be sending them anything, including their intake form or a portal invitation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the intake form to email">
    With an email address entered, **Intake form to email** appears, set to your practice's intake form. Pick another form, or **Don't send a form now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add and email the form">
    The button says what it will do: **Add and email the form**, or **Add counselee** if you're not sending one. Afterwards the screen tells you exactly what happened: the form emailed to their address and the date its link stops working, or why it couldn't be sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The email says only that a form is waiting, with no name and no practice details. Everything they fill in stays behind the link.
</Note>

If the email couldn't go out (a mistyped address, say), the screen shows the link itself once so you can send it another way. Otherwise, correct the address on their record and send the form again from the **Intake** tab.

## What the intake form fills in

When the counselee submits their form, it fills in the record's **blank** details itself: date of birth, address, phone and the rest. It never overwrites anything already on the record. Where an answer differs from what's there, the record offers the change and you choose whether to take it.

## Who they belong to

- **A counselor** who adds a counselee gets a case for them straight away, with themselves as counselor, so the new counselee is on their caseload at once.
- **An administrator or intake staff** who adds a counselee creates the record only. Start a case from the record and assign a counselor; until then, no counselor can see them.

See [Cases and statuses](/counselees/counselee-statuses) and [Assigning counselors](/counselees/assigning-counselors).

## Profile photo

On the record's **Details** tab, under **Identity**, click **Change photo** to add a picture that helps your team recognize them (PNG, JPEG, WEBP or an iPhone photo, up to 512 KB). The photo is for staff only.

## Nothing is deleted

There's no way to delete a counselee. When the counseling ends, discharge their cases; if the record shouldn't be in your lists at all (a duplicate, say), **Archive** it from the **Details** tab. Archiving is undone with **Unarchive**, and every note, session and form is still there.
