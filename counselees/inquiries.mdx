---
title: "Inquiries"
description: "People who reach out through your website, and how they get to a counselor."
---

An **inquiry** is someone who has filled in your practice's public inquiry form: their name, contact details and a message about what they'd like help with. They arrive as a **prospective** counselee.

## Switching the form on

An administrator sets this up in **Practice → Intake → Website form and routing**:

- **Accept inquiries from the public form** switches the form on or off.
- **Send unassigned inquiries to** chooses a counselor to receive every inquiry from the practice-wide link, or **Nobody — hold them for admin triage**.
- **Create a practice-wide link** makes the link you put on your website. You can also make a link for one counselor with **Or create one for**.

## A counselor's own link

A counselor can make their own link in **Practice → Intake → Your inquiry link** (**Create my link**) and put it on their page of the practice website. Anyone who fills it in arrives straight on that counselor's caseload, with no triage step.

## Triage

Inquiries from the practice-wide link that weren't routed automatically wait in **Practice → Intake → Inquiries**, which administrators and intake staff can see. Each shows when it arrived, how to reach the person, and their message. For each one:

- **Assign to** a counselor, or
- **Dismiss** it, if it isn't someone your practice will see.

Until an inquiry is assigned, no counselor can see it. Once it is, the record and the Inquiries page both say who it went to, by whom and when.

## Accepting

The counselor an inquiry was routed to finds the person under **Counselees** as **prospective**, and decides whether to take them on with **Accept as a counselee**. Only that counselor can accept them.

From there, it's the same as a counselee you added yourself: send the intake form, book a first appointment, and start a case if there isn't one yet.

<Note>
  If your practice is read-only (a lapsed subscription, for example), the public form stops taking new inquiries until it's restored.
</Note>
