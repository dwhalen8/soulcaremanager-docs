**Practice → You** belongs to whoever is signed in. Everyone on staff has it.

| Card | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Your profile** | **Edit profile**: your photo, bio, time zone and, for a counselor, your ACBC certification details. Visible to your team |
| **Your password** | **Change password**. You're asked for your current one, and changing it signs out every other device |
| **Passkeys** | Add a passkey for this device, rename one, or revoke one you no longer have |
| **ACBC certification** | Counselors only. See [ACBC certification](/supervision/acbc-progress-tracking) |
| **Email notifications** | Counselors and administrators: whether you're emailed when a counselee returns homework or an intake form comes back |
| **Problems and feedback** | **Send feedback** to tell us about something broken or confusing, or to ask for something new |

If your practice lets counselors set their own fee or gift, your default is here too. See [Charging counselees](/billing/charging-counselees).

## Your time zone

Your own time zone decides the clock **Today** and **Schedule** are drawn on. Change it in **Edit profile** if you move. See [Which clock is which](/scheduling/which-clock).
