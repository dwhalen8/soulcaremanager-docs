**Practice → Billing → Reports** (**Open reports**) is for administrators only.

Choose a **Period** (**This month**, **Last month**, **This year** or **Last 12 months**). Everything is counted on the practice's clock, and **Export CSV** downloads the figures.

## What's on it

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Outstanding money** | What's owed now, by age (0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days), oldest first, and what's been received. These use their own fixed windows and don't follow the period chosen above |
| **Caseload** | Open cases each counselor holds right now, as primary and co-counselor, and how many counselees |
| **Sessions** | Sessions held in the period, using each session's corrected times where one was filed |
| **Attendance** | Every appointment in the period, kept, cancelled and no-show, by counselor |
| **Discharge outcomes** | Cases closed in the period, by the reason recorded at discharge |

The numbers are worked out from the records each time the page opens, so they're never out of step.

## Workload

To compare counselors side by side right now (open cases, counselees, sessions this month, the next 30 days and what's waiting on each), use **Practice → Team → Workload**.
