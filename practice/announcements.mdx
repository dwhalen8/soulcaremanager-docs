---
title: "Announcements"
description: "Tell your team or your counselees something, in the app."
---

**Practice → Announcements** shows what your practice and Soul Care Manager have announced. Everyone on staff can read it.

## Post an announcement

Administrators click **Post an announcement**, write it, and choose who sees it:

- **Staff only**: your team sees it.
- **Counselees can see this**: it also appears at the top of every counselee's **My care**.

**Take down** removes it when it's no longer true.

Announcements stay in the app. Nothing is emailed.

## From Soul Care Manager

Announcements from the Soul Care Manager team (planned maintenance, say) appear in the same list, marked as coming from the platform.
