Both are in **Practice → Records**, for administrators only.

## The audit log

**Audit log** lists every read and write of your practice's records: *Who did what, and when*. Each row shows when, who, the role they were acting in, the action, the kind of record, and the **reason given** for an administrator's read of clinical content.

Rows hold ids and kinds, never what a record says. **Reading this log is itself recorded in it.**

### Filtering

- **Action**: such as *read*, *create*, *update*, *sign*, *archive*, *export* (a download), *disclose* (a share, or an email naming someone), *login* and *logout*.
- **Resource**: the kind of record, such as *appointment*, *case*, *client* (a counselee) or *file*.
- **Who**: one person on your team.
- **From** and **To**: whole days on the practice's clock. Every time in the log is on the practice's clock too, and the page says which.

The filters are part of the page's address, so you can bookmark a search or send it to a colleague.

### What it's for

Answering the questions a practice gets asked: who opened this counselee's record, when a note was signed, whether anyone downloaded a recording. An administrator's own reads are in it too, with their reasons.

## Email history

**Email history** lists *every message this practice's software has sent, and whether it arrived*: reminders, moved and cancelled appointments, form invitations, portal invitations, payment links and receipts, notifications to staff.

Each row shows when it was sent, which message, who it went to, who it was about, and the outcome: **Sent**, **Failed** or **Not delivered**. Filter by message, outcome, counselee or dates.

It never shows an email's subject or body, because no email from Soul Care Manager carries counseling content in the first place.

For one counselee, the **Email history** card on their record's **Details** tab shows the same list. Sign-in and password-reset emails aren't shown there: those belong to the person's account rather than to your practice.
