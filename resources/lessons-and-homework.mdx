title: "Lessons and homework"
description: "Teach a lesson in a session and send its homework home with the counselee."
---

A **lesson** is what you teach in a session. Unlike homework, it isn't sent to the counselee: it's copied into the session, where you adapt it for the person in front of you.

## Create a lesson

In **Practice → Library → Resource library**, click **New resource** and choose **Lesson**. Write the teaching itself, then, under the lesson's bundle, add the homework and articles to offer the counselee whenever this lesson is used.

*Nothing bundled yet. Homework and articles added here travel with the lesson.*

## Use it in a session

On the session page, under **Lesson**, search for it and choose it.

- Its text is copied into the session. Your changes stay on this session and never touch the library copy.
- Its bundled homework and articles are shown first, each one ticked, under **Use this lesson**. Untick anything you don't want, then press it. Nothing is added until you do.

If something was added by mistake, take it out again; it's archived, not deleted, and **Restore** brings it back.

## Afterwards

The session record keeps the lesson as you taught it. The homework goes to the counselee's portal like any other assignment. See [Assigning homework](/resources/assigning-resources).
