The resource library is in **Practice → Library → Resource library**. Counselors and administrators use it; intake staff don't see it.

## Two libraries in one

- **Practice library**: shared with everyone at the practice. A new practice starts with a starter set of homework, such as *Put Off / Put On Journal*, *Forgiveness: A Biblical Framework* and *Daily Scripture Meditation Guide*. Administrators add to it and edit it.
- **My resources**: your own. Only you can see, edit or assign them.

Filter by **Type** (**Homework**, **Article** or **Lesson**) and by **Where** (**Practice library** or **My resources**), or search by title. Each resource has its own page; **Preview** opens it in a reading window without leaving the list.

## The three kinds

| Type | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Homework** | Something for the counselee to do between sessions: a worksheet, a journal, a study |
| **Article** | Something for the counselee to read |
| **Lesson** | What you teach in a session. Its text is copied into the session for you to adapt, and it can bundle homework and articles to offer the counselee |

## Create a resource

Click **New resource**, choose its type, give it a title and write its content. You can attach files (worksheets as PDFs, for example). Click **Save resource**.

A resource you create goes into **My resources**.

## Copying

On any resource's page:

- **Copy to my resources** makes your own copy of a practice resource to change as you like. The original isn't touched.
- **Copy into the practice library** (administrators) publishes one of your own resources for everyone at the practice.

## Keeping your own resources private

Your own resources stay yours. A lesson only bundles material that everyone who can read the lesson can open, so a practice lesson never carries someone's private worksheet with it.

## Linking a book

Homework can be linked to a book on your practice's [lending library](/book-library/managing-your-inventory) shelf. Assigning that homework then offers to lend the book too. See [Books as homework](/book-library/books-as-homework).
