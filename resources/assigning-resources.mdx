---
title: "Assigning homework"
description: "Give a counselee homework, see what they turned in, and close it."
---

Homework is assigned from a counselee's record, or during a session.

## Assign from the record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Homework tab">
    On the counselee's record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Assign homework">
    Choose a worksheet or lesson from **Assign from the library**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Assign" />
</Steps>

In a session, assign homework the same way from the counselee's own record on the session page.

## What the counselee receives

The homework appears in their portal, and they get an email saying they have new homework and to sign in to see it. **The email never contains the homework itself**, or anything about the counseling: no title, no attachment, no content.

## Turned in, then closed

A counselee's **done** means *turned in*. They mark it done in their portal and can write a note about it, which you see on the record as **In his words** (or her, or their).

It then waits for you, *Marked done by the counselee · waiting for you to confirm*, on the record's **Homework** tab and on **Today**, with:

- **Confirm done**: it's closed.
- **Not done yet**: it goes back to them as still open.
- **Withdraw**: you're taking it back. It's withdrawn, not deleted.

You can also close homework yourself, without waiting for them to mark it, if you've seen it's done.

From **Today**, **Discuss in session** takes a turned-in item off your Today list without closing it, so you can talk it through with them first.

## In the session

The session page lists each counselee's open homework, and anything closed during that session. Earlier closed homework doesn't clutter it.

## Who can see it

The counselors on the counselee's cases, and the counselee. In a couple or family case, each person's homework is their own.
