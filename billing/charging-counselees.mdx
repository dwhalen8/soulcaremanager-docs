title: "Charging counselees"
description: "Session fees or gifts, charges and payments, card payments and statements."
---

Soul Care Manager keeps a simple account for each counselee: what they've been charged, what they've paid, and what they owe. The balance is worked out from those each time, never typed in.

## Fee or gift: Practice → Billing → Billing settings

An administrator decides how sessions are paid at the practice:

- **By fee**: each session is billed, at the **Standard session fee**.
- **By gift**: no bill is raised, and counselees are invited to give. A **Suggested gift per session** is never owed: it isn't on a balance or a statement, and nobody is reminded of it.

A case can be set either way on its own page, and a fee set on a case always wins. With **Counselors may set their own** on, each counselor can choose fee or gift as their own default (in **Practice → You**) and on their cases.

A case fee of $0 means pro bono: no charge is raised.

**Raise a charge automatically when a session is completed** creates the charge only. No money is ever taken automatically.

## The counselee's Billing tab

On the counselee's record, **Billing** shows their **Balance**, any card on file, their **Charges** and the **Payments received**.

- **Charge for a session** bills a session. The **⋯** menu has **Add a charge** (for anything else) and **Record a payment** (cash or check).
- **Download statement (PDF)** gives you a statement to hand over or mail. Counselees can download theirs in the portal too.

Counselors can see a counselee's balance and statement. Raising charges, recording payments, write-offs and refunds are an administrator's.

## Getting paid by card: Practice → Billing → Payment setup

To take card payments, connect your practice to Stripe: **Set up payments**, and follow Stripe's steps. The card shows whether Stripe has your details, whether you can take payments, and whether money is reaching your bank. **Check with Stripe** asks again.

Money goes straight to your practice's Stripe account. Stripe's processing fee comes out of each payment, at cost; Soul Care Manager takes nothing on top. If a payment is refunded or disputed, Stripe's fee for that is your practice's too.

## Payment links

For an unpaid charge:

- **Payment link** gives you a link to send.
- **Email payment link** emails the link to the counselee's address on file. The email is the link alone.

The counselee pays by card on a secure page. When they pay, the charge is marked paid.

## Saving a card

A counselee can save their card while paying, so you can charge it later, but only once your practice publishes **Card-on-file terms** (**Practice → Billing**). Start from the suggested wording, change anything that isn't how your practice works, and **Publish new version**. Earlier versions are kept, so a counselee's agreement always points at the text they read.

## Write-offs, voids and refunds

- **Write off** marks some or all of a charge as not to be collected. It counts as settled, never as income.
- **Void** cancels a charge raised by mistake, if nothing has been paid on it.
- **Refund** returns a card payment through Stripe, or records a cash refund, with its own date and reason.

Nothing is deleted: each of these is recorded beside the original charge or payment.

## Households

On the counselee's **People** tab, **Start a household** groups people who live as one unit, so their bills can go to one person. In a couple or family session, the charge goes to whoever receives that household's bills.

## Reports

**Practice → Billing → Reports** shows outstanding money alongside caseload, sessions and attendance. See [Reports](/practice/reports).
