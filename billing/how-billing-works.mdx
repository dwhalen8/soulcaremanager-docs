Your practice's subscription lives in **Practice → Billing → Subscription**, and only administrators see it.

## Founding practices

Practices that moved to this version of Soul Care Manager from the original one are on the **Founding Practice** plan. Nothing is limited, and there's nothing to pay or set up. Founding Practice is set by Soul Care Manager, not from this screen.

## The plans

| Plan | Price | For |
|---|---|---|
| **Practice** | $29 per counselor a month, or $290 per counselor a year (two months free) | Most practices |
| **Ministry** | $59 a month, or $590 a year, for any number of counselors | Churches and registered nonprofits |

On the Practice plan, a **counselor** is anyone on your team who holds the Counselor role. Administrators and intake staff who don't counsel aren't counted. When you invite a counselor, the invitation says what it will add to your bill, and the change is invoiced straight away.

The Subscription page shows your plan, how many people are on your team and how many are counselors, and how much you're storing.

## Paying

Payment is taken by **Stripe**, and Stripe sends the invoices and receipts. Your card details go to Stripe, never to us.

## Free trials

A new practice can start with a 30-day trial of the Practice plan (when sign-up is open). See [Starting a free trial](/getting-started/starting-a-free-trial).

## If a subscription lapses

If a trial ends unpaid, or a subscription is cancelled and runs out, your practice becomes **read-only**:

- Nothing is lost, and nobody is locked out. Every record stays readable, and work already begun can be finished.
- New work pauses: no new counselee, case, appointment, repeating appointment, note, session or bill.
- Your public doors close for new work: the website inquiry form, booking links, and counselees' new portal messages, prayer requests and bookings.
- Money you're already owed can still be collected.

Today and the portal both say the practice is read-only. Subscribing restores everything straight away; Today's **Subscribe and restore** takes you there.

## Leaving

You can download a complete copy of your practice's records at any time: **Practice → Records → Account and records → Download all records**. That stays available while the practice is read-only.
