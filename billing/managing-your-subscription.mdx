---

Everything here is in **Practice → Billing → Subscription**, for administrators.

## Subscribe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    **Practice** or **Ministry**. See [How billing works](/billing/how-billing-works).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose monthly or yearly" />
  <Step title="Click Continue to payment">
    Stripe's checkout page opens. Enter your card there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you come back, the page shows your plan and when it renews. A practice has one subscription: subscribing again doesn't create a second one.

## Update your card and see invoices

Click **Manage billing — card and invoices**. Stripe's billing page opens, where you can change the card, download invoices and see past payments.

If a payment fails, the Subscription page says so and asks you to update the card there.

## Cancel

Cancel here, on the Subscription page, not in Stripe.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Cancel subscription…" />
  <Step title="Tell us why">
    Choose a reason, and add a comment if you'd like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm" />
</Steps>

*The practice keeps working until the end of the period already paid for, and nothing more is charged.* Until that day, the page shows when it ends, and **Keep the subscription** undoes the cancellation.

After that day, the practice becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted, and subscribing again restores it.

## Changing plan or adding counselors

Adding or removing a counselor changes a Practice-plan bill straight away, and Stripe invoices the difference. To move between plans, get in touch.
