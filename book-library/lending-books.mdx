---
title: "Lending books"
description: "Lend a book to a counselee, and record its return."
---

Books are lent from the counselee's record, on the **Homework** tab, under **Books**.

## Lend a book

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Lend a book" />
  <Step title="Find the book">
    Search by title or author, then choose it under **Book**. The list says how many copies are available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a due date and notes (optional)">
    **Due back** and **Notes** are both optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save" />
</Steps>

The book now shows under **Currently checked out**, and one fewer copy is available on the shelf.

## When it comes back

Click **Returned** beside it. If it's gone for good, **Mark lost**.

A book given as a gift shows as **Gifted** in their history, and isn't expected back.

## History

Every loan stays in the counselee's book history, so you can see what they've read. On the book's own page, the practice can see its loans too.

## Overdue

A loan past its due date counts toward **Books out too long** in **Practice → Library**.

## Who can see who has a book

Who has borrowed what names a counselee, so it's part of their record: the counselors on their cases see it, and an administrator gives a reason to see it, which is recorded.
