---
title: "Books as homework"
description: "Link homework to a book on your shelf, so assigning one lends the other."
---

Reading a book is often the homework. Soul Care Manager lets you link the two, so you don't have to do it twice.

## Link a book to homework

Open the homework resource in **Practice → Library → Resource library**, and choose the book under its linked book (*No linked book* until you do). *Assigning this homework will offer to loan the book too.*

## Assigning it

When you assign that homework to a counselee, you're offered to lend them the book at the same time. Say no if they already have a copy.

## Returning the book

When the book comes back and you mark it **Returned**, the reading homework is completed with it, as long as you're someone who could have closed that homework yourself.

## If there's no copy on the shelf

The offer to lend says so. Assign the homework anyway, and lend the book later from the counselee's **Homework** tab when a copy comes back.
