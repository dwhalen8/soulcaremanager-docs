---
title: "The lending library"
description: "Keep track of the physical books on your practice's shelf."
---

The **lending library** is the practice's shelf of physical books, to lend or give to counselees and staff. Find it in **Practice → Library → Lending library**.

Everyone on staff can see the shelf. Administrators add and change books; counselors lend them.

## The shelf

Each book shows its title, author and how many copies are on the shelf, for example *2 available of 3*. Filter by category (**Counseling**, **Marriage & Family**, **Anxiety & Fear**, **Depression & Grief** and others) or search by title or author.

## Add a book

- **Add from catalog** finds the book in a catalog of published books and fills in its details and cover.
- **Add a book** lets you type the details yourself.

For each book you set its title, author, ISBN, **Category**, a **Description**, **Copies owned**, and **Keep on the shelf**: how many copies to hold back so there's always one to lend. Giving a copy away never takes the shelf below that number.

## Changing or retiring a book

Open the book's page to change its details or copies (**Edit this book**). A book you no longer keep can be **Archived**; its loan history is kept, **Include archived** shows it on the shelf list again, and **Restore to the shelf** brings it back.

## Overdue books

**Practice → Library** has a **Books out too long** card saying how many books are overdue. Because who has a book names a counselee, an administrator gives a reason to see who has them, and the counselors are told.
